## Tagging Guide

### MOC

Most **Folder Notes** must be tagged as `#moc` or Maps of Content. MOCs must be used to summarize or list the contents within it.

### Characters

**Characters** are tagged as `#character`. It has two subtags: `#character/npc` and `#character/pc`.

### Meta

`meta` is for notes that are about the vault itself.

### Items

Items are tagged as the following:

- The main tags are `#magic_item` and `#equipment`.
- Under Equipment, `/gear`, `/weapon`, `/armor`, `/tool`, `/gem`, `/ammunition`, `/focus`, `/mount` (includes vehicles), `/consumable`.
- Under Magic Item, `/wondrous`, `/armor`, `/weapon`, `/consumable`.

## Campaign Structuring

Campaigns must go into their own individual folder within their respective World Folder. The Main Folder must be turned into an introductory note as well as function as an MOC. Inside the folder must be the Player Characters as well as a folder named Sessions, where all DM Session Notes for the Campaign must be kept. Use [[[Veneris Campaign]] as an example.
