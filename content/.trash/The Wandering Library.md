**Beginning**:
My name is Eliana Nichols. I was never that interested in books from when I was a child, but my sister was. I've idolized her for as long as I've known her, so when she died in the fall of last year, I ended up responsible for her business.
Her library, when I got to it, was dusty. It's a small cozy hut built into the back of a small tortoise. The roof was hard and sturdy but overgrown with moss. It was a bit of a challenge to get inside, but once I was there, I was met with a tall ceiling, the space filled entirely with bookshelves. My sister put so much love into the place that I couldn't help but shed a few tears. This place is so hers. My favorite part of the Library ended up being a small bay window near the back of it. One of the throw pillows was the one that I gifted her few Christmases ago. The reindeer stares back at me.
