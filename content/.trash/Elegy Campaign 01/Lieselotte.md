Liese had a terrible drug addiction when she was alive. One night, when taking pills with some other people, she ends up overdosing. One of her friends had turned her so she doesn't end up dying.

Progenitor: Alya
Ages: Apparently 24, Really 29
Looks and Vibe: Liese still likes to dress up whenever she goes out.
Possessions: Clothes scatter her abandoned home. She always wear a necklace given to her by her best friend.
Home: Liese usually sleeps in whatever bar or club she can sneak her way into. But, if none, she sleeps in an old dilapidated building near the outskirts.
Occupation in Life: DJ

##### Artistry
*Feats appropriate for Aspects that reflect artistic  ability and sensibility.*
 - [ ] When you respite by creating art, roll with the  appropriate attribute. On a Stylish Success with  a match, it brings back memories from when  you were alive: trace ¼ diamond on Humanity.
 - [ ] When you perform or present art to someone,  roll with the appropriate attribute. On any  Success, add your dots when interacting with  anyone in your audience until the scene ends  or you somehow upset them.
 - [ ] When you analyze people based on the art  and entertainment they consume, or on their  aesthetic preferences, add +1.
 - [ ] When you roll a match of 9s or 0s, you’re struck  by a surge of inspiration related to the events,  places or characters of this scene: increase your  base Rush until you slumber.
 - [ ] When you spend a night creating art inspired  by someone you share a connection with, and  get any Success, you may gift the piece to them:  mark progress on the connection. You may  do this once per connection. Then, when you  fulfill the connection, trace +¼ diamond on its  respective experience track.
