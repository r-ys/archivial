# Reunion
[[Soleus]] and [[Lune]] are soulmates in every sense of the word. They are lovers, friends, siblings, and enemies, always have been and always will be. They are held together by destiny, both souls yearning for the other.

A **reunion** happens when the sun crosses the moon in the sky, or vice versa. These are called solar or lunar reunions respectively, also known as eclipses in the Common tongue. In these few seconds, the two meet and the world is plunged into darkness, going back to the times when the sun nor the moon were present in the sky, but Soleus and Lune could spend all the time they want together. These reunions happen at random times, with neither scholars nor arcanists fully aware of the explanation behind them.
