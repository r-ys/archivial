Campaign in Veneris of witch-hunters announced on May 24, 2025.

#### Concept:

You are all soldiers part of the Sacred Order in Veneris, specializing in different branches of the organization. However, because of the growing fear against witches in the kingdom, a new branch is formed, Absolution, which is focused on the killing and extermination of witches in Veneris and the surrounding territories. Unfortunately, through no control of your own, you are reassigned to a new team under this branch. You have been assigned to investigate and destroy witches and their covens around the area.
