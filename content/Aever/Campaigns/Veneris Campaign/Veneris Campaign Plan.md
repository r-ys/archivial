## Premise

The Absolution Branch of the Sacred Order has just been created, pulling some soldiers off of their respective branches and into a witch hunter team. This is the party.

**The Crime:**

In the port area of Veneris, there's been a number of murders that have happened over the span of the last few months.

**Victims:**

1. two children, dead in a house fire,
2. entire crew, killed during a banquet through poison,
3. high ranking noble, killed in his bed,

**planned to kill but hasnt yet:**

4. noble #2, FUCKED UPP, has foreign elves tied up in his basement DAFAQ,
5. one of the generals in the sacred order

The thing all these guys have in common is that they're a part of a hidden trafficking organization. This trafficking org and the mercenary group had a deal that the former did not follow up on. So, they sent Spica to Veneris, the traffickers' main hub, to send a message to them and destroy what they have.

## Spica

[[Spica]] is an 83 year old **owlin witch**. She is allied with Baron's old mercenary group. She has made her home in one of the abandoned ships docked at the Veneris port, where she came in as a stowaway. She's a new member of Baron's old mercenary group, very new. This mission is actually one of her initiations.

The house that was burned down was the target. It is owned by the secretary of the traffickers and contains important documents. Spica burned it down to destroy these documents, but she did not account for the children that were left inside. Those murders were collateral. It was her first act in the job, and made a horrifying mistake. She won't repeat that ever again.

The entire crew of a ship was killed during one of their get-togethers at one of the taverns. Spica was staying in the same tavern in one of the rooms directly above, but could see what was happening through her cat **Azimech**. Spica administered the poison through the spell Unseen Servant. The crew all just slumped over the table one by one and died, except for one of the Praedaren that didn't drink that night because blud is two years sober.

The most recent murder. The high ranking noble was strangled in his sleep in his estate.

## Player Backstories

### Akiro and Akira Locke

The Locke twins were separated at 12. Monsters had attacked their village, and their family split up; Akira with their dad, and Akiro with their mom. Their father ran straight to Veneris City, where they managed to find sanctuary. However, Akiro and their mom went the opposite way, to the forest, and became severely injured in the process. That was when they stumbled upon [[witch coven]]. With the mother's dying breath, she entrusted her son with the witches and asked them to save him. The coven took Akiro and performed the ritual to make him a witch in order to save him. For the next 10 years, Akiro grew up as a witch with the coven, and Akira in Veneris.

Both reunited at 22 from being assigned to the same Sacred Order team.

**Knives**:

- Dad (non making)

- dead? mom

### Baron Quenton

Baron was originally part of a roaming mercenary group called the [[Howling Iron]]. He was good at it, doing all sorts of jobs of varying legality. That is until his group was given a job that unfortunately resulted in the deaths of a mother and son. The event traumatized him and led him to quit altogether. He went off the grid a few years until he finds the kill signs of his merc group popping up near him, so he decides to join up in the Sacred Order with a new name and a new face.

### Iris

Iris's patron is an archwitch that she has devoted her life to.
