## [[Anastasis]]
Campaign centered on [[Eternals]] as a concept. Using the Daggerheart System. The players play as eternals that are going through anastasis or the process of rebirth. They are in Aever's version of purgatory and must adventure through it to retrieve their souls and reincarnate.
