The **Sacred Order** is a group of knights recruited by the [[Veneris City|Venere royal clergy]]. They are the main combative group assigned to deal with various 'sacrilegious' and 'unholy' threats in the entirety of Veneris. There are different divisions within the organization dedicated to certain things, such as the undead, vampires, or witches.

The different branches are as follows:
- **Culling** - assigned to vampires and werewolves.
- **Absolution** - assigned to terminating witches. It is the most recently formed branch of the Order due to the heightening panic towards witches.
- **Deliverance** - assigned to devils and demons. This branch contains some of the most powerful knights.
- **Dissipation** - assigned to minor troublemaking spirits.
- **Adherence** - assigned to the criminals and the delinquents. They act like a kind of police force in the kingdom, its members spiritually the farthest to the Order.
