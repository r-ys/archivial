Eternals are beings forced into reincarnating. Their souls are always in the loop of life. As soon as they die, their souls are born again in a different body, each life different than the last.

Curses are the only way to become an eternal, and only that version of them is where they know it's an eternal. The moment that they die, their hair and body turn into a bright white, and their corneas go black. The body never ever decomposes until the original soul happens to ascend. The corpse will always look fresh, whatever happens.

There are two versions of the curse. One where they curse your mother to give birth to one, and one where you yourself are cursed. The version where your mother gives birth, it only means that that eternal is one that's already in the loop. The only way to create one is to curse the person to become an eternal themselves.

Born eternals do not know they are until told. Either by the parents, or the witch/sorcerer. Then, all their past memories come back. Only the memories come back, though, and the child's personality is not undone. They can do with that information as they wish.
Most eternals want their reincarnations to stop after three to five lives. They start to miss their old friends and lovers, and maybe yearn for an earlier time. They would maybe commit suicide over and over to try and stop it, but have no idea how to do it. All of their memories from each life are specific, including the way they died.

Different cultures have different ways of dealing with these. Some worship them as gods, some treat them like normal being, while most treat them with spite and jealousy. This jealousy sometimes leads to the genocide of eternals. While they do want the loop to stop, eternals would really rather ascend painlessly and quietly. They've been through enough.
