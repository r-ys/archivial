Lune is the personified manifestation of the Moon. They have no control over their appearance, showing themself as various races, genders, and ages, and thus known to be genderless.

### Religion

**Lunaism** is the practice of worshipping Lune.
The following are some well-known groups that participate:

- [[Witches]]
- [[Nox Eternity]]
