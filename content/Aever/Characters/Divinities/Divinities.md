Divinities are the essential aspects that give the world life. They are gods in the technical sense, but they work a bit differently as they don't need the belief or support of humans to continue existing.

> Divinities are beings that are higher than gods in that they embody concepts that are essential to the known world. They are usually the personification of forces of nature. Divinities aren't technically gods in the sense that they actively interact with the world regardless of worship, though they do have religions that are focused on them.

Their are four known divinities:

- [[Soleus]] - the Sun Divine
- [[Lune]] - the Moon Divine
- [[Ydris]] - the Water Divine
- [[Terresia]] - the Earth Divine
- [[Vira]] - the Life Divine
- Some scholars speculate the existence of a [[Magic Divinity|fifth divinity]], of magic, but it has yet to be proven.
