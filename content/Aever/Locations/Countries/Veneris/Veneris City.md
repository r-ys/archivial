The capital city of **Veneris** is well-known for worshipping the Divinity [[Soleus]]. It is one of the largest cities in the southern part of the continent, with a large area of territory surrounding it. Since Veneris's patron is the Sun, the buildings are made using generous amounts of glass and windows, especially on their roofs. Balconies and skylights are a common feature of houses here. In the middle of the city stands the [[Sun Cathedral]], a large sunlamp at the top of it. During the day, the lamp collects sunlight and glows, which persists until the nighttime and provides light for any night activities.

Veneris is currently ruled by [[Ysaac Aris II]].
