These pieces of jewelry cast an undetectable pre-programmed Seeming spell that targets the wearer, specifically those that are witches. The spell makes the wearer look like a normal being, removing the hallmarks of being a [[Witches|witch]], namely the nightsky skin and their soulstar. This effect lasts for only 8 hours, then the item slowly powers down and reveals the truth underneath.
